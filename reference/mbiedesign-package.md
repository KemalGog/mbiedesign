# mbiedesign: Model-based cancer screening trial design

Model-based design of cancer screening trials that use late-stage cancer
incidence as the endpoint. A calibrated multistate natural-history
model, fit by maximum likelihood to population incidence data, is used
to project stage-specific trial outcomes under the traditional and
intended-effect (IE) designs, and to compute the per-arm sample size and
relative efficiency of each design.
